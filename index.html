How this report was built

Source: backend export UpCareer Client Report 20260731.xlsx (Full User List + 01–05 tabs), 101 total accounts.
  • Real players (65) excludes 24 internal/staff accounts (LoveXpress team domains and Kevin's own logins, used for QA) and 12 obvious test/QA accounts (e.g. "Test Account", "mactest001", "pp@aa.com"). Use the toggle above to see the unfiltered 101.
  • "Time to complete" is wall-clock time between first and last question, not necessarily active screen time — a handful of sessions spanning 5–120 hours suggest the game was left open rather than played continuously across days. Median is used as the headline figure for that reason; both are shown below.
  • "Where the game stops" is derived by matching each in-progress player's decision count to the actual sequence of decisions logged for other players, so the stage names (e.g. "Broken Water Pump") reflect the real question order rather than a guess.
  • SEN self-identification comes directly from each player's own answer to the first in-game question ("Identify autistic level") — it's a self-report, not a clinical assessment, and "Not yet reached this question" covers anyone who signed in but hasn't gotten that far (or never signed in at all).
  • "Help Clicks" in the raw export was populated for only 2 of 101 accounts and isn't reliable enough to report on yet.
  • Not covered in this report, but available in the raw data for a follow-up: the post-game player survey (05-surveydata, 42 respondents rating game length, content, and engagement) and the 24-capability / 8-attitude scoring behind each completed profile (02/03 tabs).
